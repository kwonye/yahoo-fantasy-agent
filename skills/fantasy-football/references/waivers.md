# Waivers, FAAB, and acquisitions

Read league waiver type, claim processing times, waiver order, FAAB balance, move limits, roster capacity, and pending claims. Determine whether a target is a free agent or on waivers in this league; global ownership percentage is a market signal, not availability.

Compare the target with the actual player displaced and the best available alternative. Include starting utility, role durability, upside, roster depth, bye coverage, competitive horizon, and opportunity cost. Short-lived injury fill-ins and sustained role changes deserve different valuations. Protect keeper/dynasty assets from redraft-only drop logic.

Build ranked contingency claims with explicit add/drop pairs. Check whether multiple successful claims could overspend, leave a lineup hole, or repeatedly drop the same player. Yahoo's claim ordering and conflict behavior govern execution; do not assume pending bids are already deducted from the displayed balance. Recheck after processing and avoid duplicating existing claims.

Size bids from the improvement over replacement, player market, remaining weeks, saved cap/reserve, and competing needs. A percentage of the original budget is not interchangeable with the current balance. Do not use a universal fixed bid table or spend the entire budget because authority permits it. Explain aggressive bids in context, especially late-season bids when unused FAAB has little future value.

For free agents, prefer an atomic add/drop over a separate drop followed by an add. Check undroppable/protected players and lineup lock restrictions. Evaluate legal IR moves before sacrificing useful depth. Stream defenses, kickers, and suitable IDP roles only when the expected improvement exceeds roster and transaction cost.

Use journaled preparation/execution for claims, bid/priority edits, cancellations, and pickups. A verified pending claim is only a confirmed submission. Follow up after Yahoo processing; report won/lost/failed outcomes accurately, update available alternatives, and preserve the same decision IDs when reconciling a lost response.
