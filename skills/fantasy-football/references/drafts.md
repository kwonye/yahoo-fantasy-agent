# Draft preparation and live advice

Read draft type, team count, roster/scoring, keeper rules, available budget or pick position, existing keepers, and player availability. Clarify hidden rules before applying assumptions. Prepare tiers and contingent roster plans, not a rigid round-by-round list that ignores the board.

Value players over replacement in the actual roster format. Adjust QB scarcity for superflex/2QB, receptions and tight-end premiums, IDP scoring/required starters, and bench depth. In keeper/dynasty contexts incorporate retained-player cost, age, development, and competitive horizon. Separate current-season production from long-term value.

For snake drafts, compare tier drop-offs, likely availability before the next pick, positional runs, and roster needs. Do not react to a run by reaching beyond the value of available alternatives. Use ADP as evidence about the market, not as the definition of player value.

For auctions, reserve the minimum legal funds for remaining roster spots, update budgets after each purchase, account for keeper inflation and remaining player supply, and set walk-away prices. Explain when a nomination is about testing the market or shaping opponents' budgets. Never exceed legal remaining spending capacity.

During live advice, refresh the board and confirm elapsed picks before recommending. Present a preferred choice with a small number of realistic alternatives and the condition that would change the choice. The MCP does not submit live draft picks or bids; clearly label recommendations and do not claim to have drafted anyone.
