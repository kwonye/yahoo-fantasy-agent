# Season and format strategy

## Competitive horizon

Use standings, remaining matchups, playoff qualification rules, scoring tiebreakers, and the saved goal. A contender may exchange speculative bench value for dependable starts; a rebuilding dynasty team may make the opposite trade. Do not infer a rebuilding mandate from a short losing streak when the saved goal is balanced.

For redraft, weigh immediate production against rest-of-season upside. For keeper leagues, apply actual keeper cost and retention limits. For dynasty, include durable workload, development paths, age curves, and liquidity without treating youth alone as value. Protected-player preferences override speculative optimization.

## Format adjustments

PPR, half-PPR, standard, premium positions, yardage bonuses, and negative scoring can change close comparisons. Recompute from compatible component projections when available. Superflex/2QB creates different quarterback scarcity and backup value from single-QB leagues. Large benches and deep leagues reduce replacement quality.

For IDP, use the league's individual defensive positions and scoring. Distinguish tackle-driven volume from sack/turnover dependence; compare snap rate, alignment, role, and eligibility. A generic overall IDP ranking may not fit a league separating defensive tackles, edges, linebackers, corners, and safeties. Do not substitute team-defense guidance for individual defenders.

## Playoffs and offseason

As playoff odds and seeding become clearer, compare immediate qualification needs with playoff matchups, late-season bye exposure, depth, and high-value contingencies. Do not stash for playoffs at the cost of a necessary current-week lineup. Revisit remaining FAAB and claim strategy as the number of useful weeks shrinks.

After the season, summarize decisions and results with context: injuries, news changes, and outcome variance do not by themselves prove the original reasoning was bad. Identify recurring data gaps or strategic mistakes, update the user's preferences only with their direction, and prepare draft/keeper research.

Offseason host checks default to daily when enabled. Once Yahoo advances to a new game/season, discover it, preserve historical journals, and obtain enrollment for the new team key. Never transfer prior-season authority automatically.
